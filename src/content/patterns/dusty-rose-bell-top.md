## Why you will love it

A floaty bell-shaped top with a puff-stitch bodice and hand-shaped roses along the hem. This one is written the way I would explain it to a friend sitting next to me - every round counted, every shaping step spelled out, and no surprises at the finish line.

> Skill level: **intermediate** - Hook **4.0 mm (G)** - Yarn **DK (3)**

## Materials

- Cotton-blend DK yarn in Dusty Rose - 5 skeins
- 4.0 mm hook
- Tapestry needle
- 6 stitch markers
- 2 m of 6 mm satin ribbon

## Gauge

18 sts x 10 rows = 4 in (10 cm) in dc. Take the two minutes to swatch - with dk (3) yarn a half-stitch difference compounds fast.

## Abbreviations

| Short | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ch | chain |
| sc | single crochet |
| hdc | half double crochet |
| dc | double crochet |
| sl st | slip stitch |

| st(s) | stitch(es) |

## Notes before you start

- Written in **US crochet terms**.
- The turning chain does *not* count as a stitch unless stated.
- Mark the first stitch of every round; it saves a lot of counting later.
- Read the whole pattern once before you pick up the hook.

## Instructions

1. Ch 96 (98, 104, 110, 116, 122) and join with a sl st, taking care not to twist.
2. Rnd 1-4: work dc evenly around; the bodice stays perfectly straight here.
3. Rnd 5-18: alternate one puff round with one dc round to build the textured panel.
4. Rnd 19: increase 8 sts evenly - this is where the bell begins to open.
5. Rnd 20-42: repeat the increase round every 3rd round until the hem measures 46 in (117 cm).
6. Rnd 43: finish with a picot edge to soften the flare.

## Finishing

- Make 9 roses: ch 42, work 2 dc in each st, then coil and secure with a few stitches.
- Sew the roses along the lower left of the bell in a loose diagonal.
- Weave the ribbon through Rnd 3 for adjustable straps.
- Block gently with steam - the bell drapes much better afterwards.

## Make it yours

Swap the main colour for anything in the same weight - this shape carries a bold shade just as well as it carries dusty rose. If you make one, tag it so I can see it; nothing beats seeing a pattern out in the world.
