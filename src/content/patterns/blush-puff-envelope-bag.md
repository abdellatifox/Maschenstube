## Why you will love it

A structured clutch worked in dense puff stitch with a magnetic snap and a plush foldover flap. This one is written the way I would explain it to a friend sitting next to me - every round counted, every shaping step spelled out, and no surprises at the finish line.

> Skill level: **easy** - Hook **5.0 mm (H)** - Yarn **Worsted (4)**

## Materials

- Worsted cotton in Blush - 2 skeins
- 5.0 mm hook
- Magnetic snap 18 mm
- Cotton lining fabric 12 x 20 in
- Tapestry needle

## Gauge

12 puffs x 12 rows = 4 in (10 cm). Take the two minutes to swatch - with worsted (4) yarn a half-stitch difference compounds fast.

## Abbreviations

| Short | Meaning |

## Notes before you start

- Written in **US crochet terms**.
- The turning chain does *not* count as a stitch unless stated.
- Mark the first stitch of every round; it saves a lot of counting later.
- Read the whole pattern once before you pick up the hook.

## Instructions

1. Ch 34. Row 1: sc in 2nd ch from hook and across (33 sts).
2. Row 2: ch 1, then work puff, sc alternately to the end.
3. Rows 3-40: repeat Row 2, keeping the puffs stacked in columns.
4. Rows 41-56: continue for the flap, decreasing 1 st at each end every 4th row.

## Finishing

- Fold the first 20 rows up and seam the sides with sc for a visible edge.
- Sew in the lining, then fit the magnetic snap 1 in below the flap point.
- Optional: add a 48 in chained strap for a crossbody version.
