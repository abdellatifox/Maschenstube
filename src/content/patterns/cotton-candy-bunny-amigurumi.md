## Why you will love it

A pocket-sized bunny with long floppy ears, a chunky bow and safety eyes - a perfect gift make. This one is written the way I would explain it to a friend sitting next to me - every round counted, every shaping step spelled out, and no surprises at the finish line.

> Skill level: **easy** - Hook **3.0 mm (D)** - Yarn **DK (3)**

## Materials

- DK cotton in Cotton Candy and Cream - 1 skein each
- 3.0 mm hook
- 8 mm safety eyes
- Polyester fibrefill
- Stitch marker

## Gauge

Tight - no gaps for stuffing to show through. Take the two minutes to swatch - with dk (3) yarn a half-stitch difference compounds fast.

## Abbreviations

| Short | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ch | chain |
| sc | single crochet |

## Notes before you start

- Written in **US crochet terms**.
- The turning chain does *not* count as a stitch unless stated.
- Mark the first stitch of every round; it saves a lot of counting later.
- Read the whole pattern once before you pick up the hook.

## Instructions

1. Head: magic ring 6 sc, increase to 36 sts over 6 rounds, work 10 even rounds, then decrease.
2. Insert the safety eyes between rounds 12 and 13, nine stitches apart.
3. Body: magic ring 6 sc, increase to 30, work 12 rounds, decrease to 12 and stuff firmly.
4. Ears (make 2): ch 8, work 24 rounds of sc, leaving them unstuffed so they flop.
5. Arms and legs: small cylinders of 12 sts, 10 rounds each.

## Finishing

- Sew the head to the body with the seam facing back.
- Attach the ears at rounds 4 and 5 of the head, angled slightly outward.
- Embroider the nose in Rose using satin stitch, then tie the bow around the neck.

## Make it yours

Swap the main colour for anything in the same weight - this shape carries a bold shade just as well as it carries cotton candy. If you make one, tag it so I can see it; nothing beats seeing a pattern out in the world.
